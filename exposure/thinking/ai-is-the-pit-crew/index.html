AI is the pit crew, not the driver.

Use AI for speed: research, versions, reporting and automations. Keep the creative calls human: the brief, the casting and the taste.

In this piece

  1. What it's great at
  2. What it can't do yet
  3. Effort isn't authenticity
  4. How I run it

By Matt KingNovember 20264 min read

AI changes the tyres fast. It doesn't drive the car.

What it's great at

Research, versions, reporting and automations. Jobs that used to take an afternoon now take minutes. I use Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Make.com every week. That is the pit crew: fast, precise and essential.

What it can't do yet

It can't make the creative call. It doesn't know why a footballer in a twinset sells more menswear than an influencer does, or how to take something that worked in one market and land it in another. That is taste, and taste is the driver.

Effort isn't authenticity.

Effort isn't authenticity

When making content is cheap, audiences stop rewarding effort. They reward taste, access and truth: the real tunnel, the real reaction, the moment nobody could have planned. Content that only exists because it was easy to make tends to read that way.

How I run it

  2. 02

  3. 03
