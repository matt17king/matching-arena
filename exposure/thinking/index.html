How fans are made.

Nobody falls for 90 minutes. They fall for the story. This is how I think about getting them there.

Five ideas

01What I believe

01 / 05

The tunnel is the new runway.

Fashion is everywhere in sport. NBA tunnel fits, the F1 paddock, the walk into training: every arrival is a runway.

02 / 05

Culture is the side door.

Reach people through what they already love. Then put the sport in that world.

03 / 05

Fit beats size.

The right voice for the idea beats the biggest name that doesn't fit it. These are the eleven rules I cast talent by.

04 / 05

The format is the product.

Change the format and you change who turns up. Split surfaces, handicapped courts, smaller courts with walls, one-point shoot-outs: the sport stays, the door gets wider.

05 / 05

Story before sport.

Drive to Survive turned box-set watchers into F1 fans. They came for the people and stayed for the racing.

How a fan is made

02Seven steps

From a stranger to a fan.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    They meet it somewhere else

    Nobody discovers a sport on its own channel. They find it in a feed, a song, a fit or a friend's story. That's where I start.

  2. 02

    Curiosity

    A person, not a fixture

    Curiosity attaches to people: a personality, a rivalry, a look in the tunnel. The fixtures come later.

  3. 03

    Story

    The off-pitch story

    Drive to Survive proved it. Give people the story and they'll come for the sport.

  4. 04

    Ritual

    A reason to come back

    A story becomes a habit when there's a reason to return every week: a format, a drop, a character arc.

  5. 05

    Belonging

    Somewhere to stand

    Fans want a side and a community. The shirt, the group chat, the people who get it.

  6. 06

    Advocacy

    They bring someone

    The best media buy is a fan explaining it to a friend. Make that easy, and worth doing.

  7. 07

    Fandom

    It's part of who they are

    Now the sport turns up where they didn't expect it: what they wear, what they listen to, who they follow.

Working with talent

03Eleven rules

Fit beats size.

  1. 01

    Give them their voice

    Talent are the storytellers. My job is the brief and the platform, not their script.

  2. 02

    Fit beats size

    Creative fit beats creative size. The right voice for the idea beats the biggest name that doesn't fit it.

  3. 03

    If you script them, you picked wrong

    Needing to micromanage talent usually means the casting was off.

  4. 04

    Idols sell, influencers inform

    At The Couture Club, influencers sold the womenswear. The menswear didn't move until footballers posted it. Then it sold out.

  5. 05

    Every pick needs a reason

    A counterintuitive pick can be the best one. An unexplained one never is.

  6. 06

    Match the person to the idea

    Lamine Yamal and Bad Bunny are both huge. Neither works unless the idea fits who they are.

  7. 07

    Borrow the audience you want

    Pick partners for the people who follow them, especially the ones who don't watch the sport yet.

  8. 08

    Local heroes travel

    Lead with who each market already loves. Ohtani in Japan opens doors no global ad can.

  9. 09

    The tunnel counts

    Arrivals, fits, warm-ups, the walk into training. The off-pitch moments are content too.

  10. 10

    Test small, then scale

    Trial and error is the process. Find what works with one partner, then take it to the next.

  11. 11

    Move what worked

    The real skill is taking what worked in one place and landing it somewhere new.

Formats

05The format is the product
2007Palma de Mallorca

Battle of the surfaces

Federer v Nadal. The best on grass against the best on clay, on one court split down the middle. Same sport, a match nobody had seen before.

9%smaller on her side of the net

Battle of the sexes

Kyrgios v Sabalenka, Dubai, December 2025. Her half of the court was made about 9% smaller to even it up. Bend one rule and you hand a whole new audience a reason to tune in.

20×10metres · walls in play · always doubles

Padel

Padel took tennis, shrank the court, put the walls in play and made it doubles only. Easier to pick up, better with friends, and it brings people to a racket who never joined a tennis club.
