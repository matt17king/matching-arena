Culture is the side door.

The side door is reaching new fans through what they already love, like music, fashion and creators, and then putting the sport in that world.

In this piece

  2. The side door is already open

By Matt KingNovember 20262 min read

Nobody walks in through the front door of a sport they don't follow. So go round the side, through the music, fashion and creators they already love.

The front door is for fans

Every sport has a front door: the fixtures, the broadcast, the club's own channels. It works brilliantly for people who already care. For everyone else it's shut. They don't know the players, don't follow the league, and don't want to learn the rules before they've had any fun.

The side door is already open

People rarely get into a sport through the sport. They get in through something they already love.

  • Taylor Swift fans started watching the NFL because Taylor Swift started going to games.
  • Box-set watchers got into Formula 1 through Drive to Survive.
  • Sneaker people looked at tennis because Carlos Alcaraz previewed Travis Scott's Nike shoe.
  • Millions watch the Super Bowl for the halftime show. The music is part of the product.

People don't get into a sport through the sport.

The four questions behind every brief

This is how I build a side door. Four questions, in this order.

  1. 01

    Who do we want?

    Fans we already have, or fans we don't? The answer changes everything that follows.

  2. 02

    What do they already love?

    Music, fashion, sneakers, creators, gaming, film and TV, nightlife, food. Go where their time already goes.

  3. 03

    What do we have?

    The sport, the athletes and the stories. Usually more than we use.

  4. 04

    What does it become?

    Tunnel fits, drops and collabs, a creator series, a new format, a music moment, a local hero. And, if it works, new fans.

Three side doors that keep working

Fashion. The tunnel is the new runway. NBA arrivals, the F1 paddock, the walk into training: every arrival is a fit check, and fashion audiences watch closely.

Music. The kick-off can be a concert. An artist with a fanbase in a new market can open that market faster than any advert.

Creators and formats. Change the format and you change who turns up. Baller League built six-a-side football for streams and clips, with creators in the dugouts. Padel shrank the court and made it social. The sport stays the same and the door gets wider.

Where it goes wrong

A side door that leads nowhere. If the culture moment has no route back to the sport, you've bought attention, not fans. Every side door needs a hallway: the next clip, the next episode, the next game worth watching.

And forcing it. Put an athlete in a world that doesn't suit them and it reads as an advert. That is why the casting matters as much as the idea. More on that in idols sell, influencers inform.
