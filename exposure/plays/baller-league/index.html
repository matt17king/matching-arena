By Matt KingFootball × Creators

The play

Six-a-side football with its own rulebook, built for streams and short clips, with creators and famous faces in the dugouts.

Instead of asking young fans to come to football as it is, it rebuilt football around how they already watch: short, loud, social and made to be clipped.

Why it worked

It didn't ask young fans to sit through 90 minutes. It met them in the formats they already watch.

The format is the product. Change the format and you change who turns up.

The idea behind it

This is The side door in action. The side door is reaching new fans through what they already love, like music, fashion and creators, and then putting the sport in that world.
