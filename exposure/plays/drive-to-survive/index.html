By Matt KingF1 × Netflix

The play

F1 let the cameras into the paddock and handed the story to Netflix: the rivalries, the team bosses, the drivers as people.

Before the series, Formula 1 mostly marketed the races. The series marketed the people instead: the team bosses, the rivalries, the drivers away from the car. It found an audience that liked the people long before it liked the racing.

Why it worked

It reached people who watch long-form drama, not motorsport. They met the characters first, and then they wanted the races.

Find where your future fans already spend their hours and tell the story there. Content made for new audiences beats paid ads aimed at old ones.

The idea behind it

This is Dormant demand in action. Dormant demand is the gap between how many people care about a sport's people and how many watch the sport itself.
