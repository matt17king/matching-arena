Taylor Swift × the Chiefs

No campaign at all. Taylor Swift started turning up to watch Travis Kelce, and the broadcasts followed her.

Steal this

Culture is the side door. Ask who your new audience already follows, then put the sport in that world.

By Matt KingMusic × NFL

The play

No campaign at all. Taylor Swift started turning up to watch Travis Kelce, and the broadcasts followed her.

Nobody planned it, and that is why it worked. A huge existing fanbase suddenly had a reason to watch a sport many of them had never followed, and the broadcasts leaned in rather than ignoring it.

Why it worked

Her fans suddenly had a reason to care about a game many had never watched. Someone they already loved was in the story.

Culture is the side door. Ask who your new audience already follows, then put the sport in that world.

The idea behind it

This is The side door in action. The side door is reaching new fans through what they already love, like music, fashion and creators, and then putting the sport in that world.
