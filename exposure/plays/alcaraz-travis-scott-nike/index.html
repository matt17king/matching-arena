By Matt KingTennis × Sneakers

The play

Carlos Alcaraz, tennis's It Boy, previewing Travis Scott's Nike tennis shoe.

Tennis usually markets to tennis fans. This put one of the sport's biggest young stars inside sneaker culture, where the audience is younger, bigger and talks about the drop for weeks.

Why it worked

A sneaker drop travels further than a match report. It put a tennis player in front of the sneaker world, on that world's terms.

Treat athletes like the culture figures they are. The tunnel, the warm-up and the drop are all a stage.

The idea behind it

This is Idols sell, influencers inform in action. Influencers tell people a product exists. Idols make people want to be part of it. If you need people to buy, cast an idol.
