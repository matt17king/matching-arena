Full · 143 words

Matt King is a global sports marketing leader. As Global Marketing Manager for Football at DAZN, he leads marketing across eight football rights portfolios, including the FIFA World Cup, the UEFA Champions League, Serie A, LaLiga and the Bundesliga, in seven market zones, and brought more than three million FIFA+ fans onto DAZN. Before DAZN he co-founded 80 Four, a sport, health and lifestyle marketing agency named a finalist for Circle Networks Start Up of the Year, and grew brands including The Couture Club, American Golf and Gym King through partnerships with Phil Foden, Molly-Mae Hague, Niall Horan and Judy Murray. His work takes sport off the pitch and into culture: reaching new fans through the music, fashion and creators they already love. He is based between London and Manchester, and studied at Webber International University in Florida, where he played college soccer.
