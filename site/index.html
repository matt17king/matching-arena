30–0 — BATTLE OF THE SEXES
Change the court, change the story.
Kyrgios v Sabalenka, Dubai, December 2025. Her half of the court was made about 9% smaller to even it up. Bend one rule and you hand a whole new audience a reason to tune in.

40–0 — PADEL
Same racket DNA. Built to be social.
Padel took tennis, shrank the court, put the walls in play and made it doubles only. Easier to pick up, better with friends, and it brings people to a racket who never joined a tennis club.
20×10 METRES · WALLS IN PLAY · ALWAYS DOUBLES
GAME — THE FORMAT IS THE PRODUCT
New formats find new fans.
Change the format and you change who turns up. Split surfaces, handicapped courts, smaller courts with walls, one-point shoot-outs: the sport stays, the door gets wider.
